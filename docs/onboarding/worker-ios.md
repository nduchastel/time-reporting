# Worker guide — install on iPhone/iPad (iOS)

A short, screenshot-ready walkthrough for installing the Time Reporting app on an
iPhone and recording your first time entry. **Living doc — keep current.**

> You need: the app link from your manager, your **phone number**, and the
> **temporary PIN** your manager gave you.

## 1. Open the app in Safari
1. Tap the app link your manager sent (or type the address into **Safari**).
2. Use **Safari** — "Add to Home Screen" does not appear in Chrome on iOS.

## 2. Add it to your home screen
1. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an up-arrow) at the bottom of Safari.
2. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Tap **Add** (top-right). A **Time Reporting** icon appears on your home screen.
4. Open the app from that icon — it now runs full-screen like a normal app.

*(If a small "Add Time Reporting to your home screen" banner appears in the app,
tap **How?** for these same steps.)*

## 3. Allow the microphone
The first time you record, iOS asks for microphone permission. Tap **Allow**.
Without it, the app can't hear your time entry. (You can re-enable later in
**Settings → Time Reporting → Microphone**.)

## 4. First sign-in + pick your own PIN
1. Enter your **phone number** and the **temporary PIN** from your manager.
2. You'll be asked to **change your PIN** right away — choose a new **4–6 digit**
   PIN only you know, and confirm it. (Your manager can't see it.)
3. You're in.

## 5. Record a time entry
1. Swipe between **Check IN / Check OUT / Hours Worked / Time OFF** (you may see
   only the ones your manager enabled for you).
2. Tap the big record button and speak naturally, e.g.
   *"I worked 8 hours at Simons Property today."*
3. Review what the app understood, then tap **Submit**.

## Trouble?
- **No "Add to Home Screen":** you're not in Safari — open the link in Safari.
- **Mic not working:** Settings → Time Reporting → Microphone → On.
- **Forgot your PIN:** ask your manager to reset it; you'll set a new one on next login.
