# Worker guide — install on Android

A short, screenshot-ready walkthrough for installing the Time Reporting app on an
Android phone and recording your first time entry. **Living doc — keep current.**

> You need: the app link from your manager, your **phone number**, and the
> **temporary PIN** your manager gave you.

## 1. Open the app in Chrome
Tap the app link your manager sent (or type the address into **Chrome**).

## 2. Install it
You'll usually see one of these — either works:
- An **"Install"** banner near the bottom of the screen → tap **Install**.
- Or the Chrome menu **⋮ → Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

Confirm, and a **Time Reporting** icon is added to your home screen / app drawer.
Open it from there — it runs full-screen like a normal app.

## 3. Allow the microphone
The first time you record, Chrome asks for microphone permission. Tap **Allow**.
Without it, the app can't hear your time entry. (Re-enable later in
**Settings → Apps → Time Reporting → Permissions → Microphone**.)

## 4. First sign-in + pick your own PIN
1. Enter your **phone number** and the **temporary PIN** from your manager.
2. You'll be asked to **change your PIN** right away — choose a new **4–6 digit**
   PIN only you know, and confirm it. (Your manager can't see it.)
3. You're in.

## 5. Record a time entry
1. Swipe between **Check IN / Check OUT / Hours Worked / Time OFF** (you may see
   only the ones your manager enabled for you).
2. Tap the big record button and speak naturally, e.g.
   *"I worked 8 hours at Simons Property today."*
3. Review what the app understood, then tap **Submit**.

## Trouble?
- **No Install option:** open the link in **Chrome** (not another browser); the
  option also disappears once the app is already installed.
- **Mic not working:** Settings → Apps → Time Reporting → Permissions → Microphone.
- **Forgot your PIN:** ask your manager to reset it; you'll set a new one on next login.
